tail arm l_t = 3.3 c̄, elevator limit -20° (illustrative),
minimum static margin 5 % MAC, inertias unchanged by CG moves.

## CG sweep (power approach, CR-2144 fc 2)

* Stick-fixed neutral point: 0.470 MAC; static margin at the published CG
  (0.25 MAC): 22.0 % MAC.
* Aperiodic divergence (a real root crosses zero) at 0.448 MAC, 2.2 % MAC ahead of the neutral point: the positive M_u of the

* The phugoid is weakly damped everywhere (ζ ≈ 0.02 at the published CG) and crosses
  ζ = 0 at some CG positions for small tails, as a very slow oscillatory divergence;
